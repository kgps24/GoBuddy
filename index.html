Safety by design

Trust should be visible before the car arrives.

MileMitra’s proposed safety model combines identity confidence, trip transparency, privacy and fast access to help.

1

Trust Rings

Progressive verification for phone, work email, government ID and vehicle ownership/registration.

2

Ride PIN

Start a trip only after rider and driver confirm a rotating PIN, reducing wrong-car mistakes.

3

Live trip share

Share vehicle, co-rider, route and ETA with trusted contacts using a time-limited link.

4

Route anomaly alerts

Production backend can detect long stops, unexpected deviation and stalled rides for safety prompts.

5

Private communication

Use masked calling/chat so personal phone numbers do not need to be exposed.

6

Safety Center

Emergency call, trusted contact, trip details, report flow and support access remain one tap away.

Important: emergency integrations, background checks, ID/KYC, insurance and local transport-law requirements must be implemented with approved providers and legal review before public launch.

Help Center

Simple answers for common ride questions

How is a carpool different from a taxi?

The concept here is designed for people already travelling on a route to share available seats and reasonable trip costs. It should not be positioned as an unlicensed on-demand taxi operation.

How does the match score work?

The prototype combines route overlap, detour, timing flexibility, trust verification, rating and comfort preferences. A production system would use Google Routes data and server-side scoring.

Can I choose women-only rides?

Yes, the interface includes a preference filter. Production use should pair this with verified identity controls, clear eligibility rules and anti-abuse safeguards.

How are payments handled?

The prototype includes a Razorpay-ready checkout path plus mock mode. A production deployment needs your merchant account, backend signature verification, refunds, receipts, taxes and reconciliation.

Can I schedule recurring office rides?

Yes. The product concept supports saved commute routines and recurring ride availability, with reminders and match suggestions before the departure window.
